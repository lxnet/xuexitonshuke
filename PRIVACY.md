Privacy Policy
This extension ("leo帮你学") runs locally on your device and does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, browsing history, or user data with the developer or any third party.

- All extension data (settings, API keys, extracted questions) is stored only in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) and never leaves your device except as described below.
- The API Key and model settings you enter are used solely to call the OpenAI-compatible API endpoint that YOU configure (Base URL). They are sent only to that endpoint.
- When you enable the AI auto-answer feature, the text of quiz questions on the current page is sent to YOUR configured API endpoint for answering. No data is sent to any other servers.
- The question-font decryption runs entirely offline in your browser.
